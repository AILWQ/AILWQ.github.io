About

Teaching machines to understand language —
and the laws of the world.

Research Interests

Exploring the frontier of large language models and symbolic intelligence, at the intersection of academia and industry.

AGENT

Building conversational agents for e-commerce that can plan, reflect, and act — serving customer inquiries at the scale of hundreds of millions of users.
